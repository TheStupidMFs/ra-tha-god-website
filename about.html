WHO IS RA? / ラ・ザ・ゴッド

DISTANCE
SHAPED THE SOUND.

Ra Tha God is a New Jersey-born rapper whose music identity was shaped thousands of miles from home in Okinawa, Japan.

Music was around him early, from his mother's records to the rap his older brother played. He began making music around 2010, initially for fun. During his time in the Marine Corps, creating with friends pushed that hobby toward something more serious.

By 2016, Ra was emerging from Okinawa with a sound built around melodic hooks, laid-back confidence, R&B influence and rap that can move from lo-fi atmosphere to bass-heavy production. Japan became more than a backdrop—it became part of the perspective.

That international path now runs through releases including Taking Chances Vol. 1 and the 14-track Taking Chances Vol. 2, alongside collaborations with Young Deji, Jay Worthy and Jadakiss.
